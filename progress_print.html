Project Title
EcoTrade OS
Problem Statement (How Might We)
"How might we help local shopkeepers effortlessly manage their inventory and build customer loyalty, while motivating businesses to adopt and source from sustainable, eco-friendly suppliers?"
Proposed Solution
1. Adaptive Store Setup
A flexible workspace that scales to the shop's operational needs—giving Kirana stores and pharmacies simple daily inventory and billing tools, while offering suppliers a clear format to document their sustainability practices.
2. Low-Friction Daily Management
Eliminates tedious manual typing through quick voice-based stock logging, and introduces a simple phone-number visit tracker so merchants can reward repeat shoppers without expensive POS hardware.
3. Circular Green Incentives
Directly rewards shoppers who bring reusable bags with instant checkout discounts, and enables shopkeepers to easily discover and stock products from verified eco-conscious suppliers.
Relevance & Real-World Impact
Grassroots Retail Need
Local Kirana stores and neighbourhood pharmacies run on thin margins and manual registers. They face heavy competition from quick-commerce delivery apps and lack simple tools to track demand and retain loyal repeat customers.
Practical Environmental Action
While customers and store owners care about eco-friendly choices, there is no straightforward mechanism for small businesses to evaluate supplier sustainability, or to reward customers who practice reusable habits.
Unified Value
Bridging everyday shop operations with sustainability incentives makes going green practical and rewarding, rather than an extra compliance burden.
Feasibility & Delivery Strategy
Modular 4-Person Scope
The system divides cleanly into independent modules that our 4-member team can build in parallel: frontend shop flow, inventory database, customer loyalty logic, and the supplier sustainability rating.
Reliable Tech Stack
Built using responsive React, a lightweight backend API, and browser-native speech recognition for voice input, avoiding heavy or fragile external dependencies.
Focused Working Demo
Our goal is a tight, working core prototype: a shopkeeper logs stock, bills a customer via phone number with an eco-discount, and checks supplier green ratings. Clean, stable, and easy to demonstrate.
